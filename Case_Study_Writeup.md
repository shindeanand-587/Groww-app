# Product Case Study: FestMatch F&B
**Campus Fest Discovery & Sampling Engine for Emerging Indian Food & Beverage Brands**
*Candidate: Anand Shinde*

---

### 1. My Take on the Problem Statement
Last year, a close friend who launched a boot-strapped iced-tea startup in Pune spent three chaotic weeks trying to get sampling stalls at college festivals across Pune and Mumbai. Between November and February, college festivals are the holy grail for emerging food & beverage (F&B) brands—whether selling craft soda, cold brew, energy drinks, or clean-label chips. Getting "liquid to lips"—handing a chilled trial directly to an 18-to-22-year-old student—drives authentic word-of-mouth and triggers immediate repeat orders on quick-commerce apps like Zepto and Blinkit.

Yet the current sponsorship discovery process is painful and disorganized:
- **Arbitrary Pricing:** Student sponsorship reps quote whatever they feel like on WhatsApp. One brand is quoted ₹2.5 Lakhs while another gets the same stall for ₹50,000 simply by aggressive haggling.
- **Inflated Footfalls:** Every festival brochure boasts "60,000+ Footfall," but most of that number counts general campus transit or ticketed concert attendees who never visit brand stalls.
- **No Standardized ROI Metric:** Digital marketing has clear CPM metrics (Cost Per Thousand Impressions), but college sponsorships force marketers through 30-page PDF brochures with zero standardized benchmarks to justify budgets to founders or CFOs.
- **Logistical Surprises:** My friend arrived at a fest only to discover the venue had zero 15A power lines for his beverage chillers, spoiling hundreds of bottles and wasting his ₹70,000 spend.

**What I Verified vs. What I Assumed:**
- *Verified:* I spoke directly with my friend (D2C beverage founder) and a former student sponsorship lead from COEP Pune. I confirmed that emerging brands operate on experimental budgets of ₹30,000 to ₹1.5 Lakhs per fest, and their primary worries are exaggerated footfall numbers and sudden power/chiller restrictions on event day.
- *Assumed:* I am assuming that calculating a standardized "Cost per 1,000 Students" (CPM) and capping shortlists at 3 festivals provides enough decision clarity to turn an agonizing 3-week negotiation into a 5-minute decision.

---

### 2. What is In Scope
1. **Multi-Parameter Discovery Bar:** Filter fests by City Hub (Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR), Max Budget in ₹ (interactive slider up to ₹5,00,000), Campaign Goal (Product Sampling, Food Stall, Title/Co-Sponsor), and Minimum Footfall.
2. **Standardized Decision Cards:** Every fest card highlights verified footfall, starting package, crowd vibe tag, activation chips, and an auto-computed CPM:
   $$\text{CPM} = \left(\frac{\text{Starting Package (₹)}}{\text{Verified Footfall}}\right) \times 1,000$$
3. **F&B Logistics Specs:** Granular operational details: 15A chiller power lines, stall canopy dimensions, peak consumption rush hours, and past brand sponsors.
4. **Shortlist & Compare Drawer (Max 3):** Pin up to 3 festivals to review combined student reach, total budget, and blended portfolio CPM, alongside a side-by-side comparison matrix.
5. **Pitch Call Booking Flow:** A focused modal capturing brand name, work email, and preferred slot, with a confirmation state, booking reference ID, and 1-click campaign brief export.
6. **Curated Seed Data:** 12 realistic Indian college festivals across 4 cities spanning ₹28,000 to ₹3,80,000 in packages and 6,000 to 52,000 in footfalls.

---

### 3. What is Out of Scope
- **No Login / Auth Barriers:** Frictionless zero-login access for immediate testing.
- **No Fest-Side Admin Portal:** Student committees do not upload unverified claims; data is curated upfront.
- **No In-App Payment Escrow:** Sponsorships require custom university MOUs, NDAs, and GST invoicing; the app focuses on discovery, comparison, and pitch reservation.

---

### 4. My Solution and Why it Works
The solution is **FestMatch F&B**—a purpose-built discovery and ROI engine tailored for trade marketing leads at young Indian F&B brands.

**Why this works:**
1. **Translates Opaque Quotes into Performance Metrics:** Auto-calculated CPM lets a founder instantly evaluate a ₹35k stall at Fergusson Pune against a ₹1.4L stall at Xavier’s Mumbai using an apples-to-apples cost per student reached.
2. **Shortlist Cap of 3 Prevents Analysis Paralysis:** Rather than drowning in dozens of PDF brochures, marketers curate a focused 3-campus strategy, check combined reach, and schedule a pitch call.
3. **F&B-First Logistics Upfront:** Surfacing electrical wattage, refrigeration permissions, and peak hunger hours eliminates costly on-ground disasters before contracts are signed.
