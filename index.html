Standardized Decision Metrics & CPM Math

In traditional college sponsorships, marketing managers get opaque proposals with arbitrary quotes and exaggerated footfalls. FestMatch standardizes this with:

CPM = (Starting Package ₹ ÷ Verified Footfall) × 1,000
For example, a ₹1,40,000 stall at Malhar with 28,000 verified students has a CPM of ₹5,000 (i.e. ₹5 per student reached).
F&B Brand Benchmarks:
  • Under ₹5,000 CPM: High ROI tier — Exceptional value for liquid-to-lips beverage trials and snack sampling.
  • ₹5,000 - ₹7,000 CPM: Standard tier — Balanced mix of footfall volume, brand buzz, and pro-nite crowds.
  • Over ₹7,000 CPM: Flagship tier — Premium marquee festivals (IITs) with multi-day high-density campus residence.
